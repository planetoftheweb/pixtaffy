## Global theme: boot removes the hard-set dark class and renders the site light

**Labels:** `bug`, `design`, `dark-mode`

### Steps to reproduce

1. Set the OS/emulated color preference to light.
2. Open `https://pixtaffy.com/` at 1280×800.
3. Inspect the `<html>` class and the computed background color of `<body>`.
4. Repeat at 390×844.

### Expected

Contract **D-01**: `<html class="dark">` is hard-set, so `document.documentElement.classList.contains('dark')` must be `true` regardless of OS preference.

Contract **D-02**: the page background must be `#0d1117` / `rgb(13, 17, 23)`.

### Actual

At both viewports, `htmlClass` is `""`, `isDark` is `false`, and `prefersColorScheme` is `"light"`. The body renders the light branch with `bgHex: "oklch(0.985 0.002 247.839)"`; `htmlBgHex` is `"rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)"`, and `darkBgHits` is empty.

### Screenshot

`desktop-home.png` (also reproduced in `mobile-home.png`)

### Suggested fix (non-binding hunch)

Preserve the shell's `dark` class during app initialization, or update the governing contract if light mode is now intentional.

## Homepage keyboard focus: 8 of 12 controls lack the required brand-teal ring

**Labels:** `bug`, `a11y`, `design`

### Steps to reproduce

1. Open `https://pixtaffy.com/` at 1280×800.
2. Use Tab to focus each of the 12 controls on the page.
3. Inspect each focused control's outline and box shadow.

### Expected

Contract **A-01**: every focusable control must show an `outline: none` plus a 1–2px focus ring in brand teal `#009EAA` / `rgb(0, 158, 170)`.

### Actual

Only 4 of 12 controls have a teal ring: `PixTaffy`, `Changelog`, `Releases`, and `Feedback`. The other 8 do not: `Log In`, `Sign Up`, both `Create your first image` controls, `Explore features`, `Pricing`, `Log in`, and `create an account`. The two hero CTAs with `outlineStyle: "none"` fall back to fully transparent box shadows; the remaining controls use non-teal browser outlines.

### Screenshot

`desktop-home-focus.png`

### Suggested fix (non-binding hunch)

Apply the shared brand-teal focus-ring recipe to every interactive variant, including CTA, auth, pricing, and inline-link styles.

## Homepage responsive controls: account links and header actions fall below 44×44px

**Labels:** `bug`, `a11y`, `mobile`

### Steps to reproduce

1. Open `https://pixtaffy.com/` at 390×844.
2. Measure every visible `button`, `a`, `[role="button"]`, input, select, and summary hit area.
3. Repeat at 1280×800.

### Expected

Contract **A-02**: touch targets must be at least `44×44px`; the 44px floor must hold at the mobile breakpoint.

### Actual

At 390×844, `PixTaffy` measures `114.5×40px`, `Log in` measures `47.8×22.8px`, and `create an account` measures `145.4×22.8px`. At 1280×800, `Sign Up` measures `93×38.8px`, `Log in` measures `47.8×22.8px`, and `create an account` measures `145.4×22.8px`.

### Screenshot

`mobile-home.png`

### Suggested fix (non-binding hunch)

Give these controls a minimum 44px hit area with responsive padding or a pseudo-element while preserving their visual size.

## Homepage elevation: CTA and floating-control shadows compute fully transparent

**Labels:** `bug`, `design`, `polish`

### Steps to reproduce

1. Open `https://pixtaffy.com/` at 1280×800.
2. Inspect the computed `box-shadow` for `Log In`, `Sign Up`, both `Create your first image` CTAs, and the floating 44×44px icon chips.
3. Repeat at 390×844.

### Expected

Contract **L-03**: in-flow cards are flat with `box-shadow: none`, while floating icon buttons use `shadow-lg` and primary CTAs use `shadow-lg shadow-brand-teal/20`. Contract **B-01** likewise requires a non-`none` primary-CTA shadow containing teal.

### Actual

At both viewports, `Log In`, `Sign Up`, and both `Create your first image` CTAs compute only fully transparent `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0) 0px 0px 0px 0px` shadow layers. All 15 measured `borderedShadowed` nodes, including the six floating 44×44px icon chips, report three fully transparent zero-size shadow layers.

### Screenshot

`desktop-home.png`

### Suggested fix (non-binding hunch)

Check the compiled shadow color variables/utilities and restore non-transparent elevation only for CTAs and floating controls; keep in-flow cards flat.

## Homepage links: five hover transitions snap at 0s instead of 150–200ms

**Labels:** `design`, `polish`

### Steps to reproduce

1. Open `https://pixtaffy.com/` at 1280×800.
2. Hover `Log in`, `create an account`, `Changelog`, `Releases`, and `Feedback`.
3. Inspect their computed transition duration and compare them with the other visible controls.

### Expected

Contract **M-01**: the default color/hover transition is `150–200ms` with the default ease; spot-checked controls should compute to `0.15s` or `0.2s`.

### Actual

The control-duration distribution is `{"0.15s":7,"0s":5}`. `Log in`, `create an account`, `Changelog`, `Releases`, and `Feedback` each declare `transitionProperty: "all"` but compute to `transitionDuration: "0s"`, so their hover changes snap while the other seven controls ease over `0.15s`.

### Screenshot

`desktop-home.png`

### Suggested fix (non-binding hunch)

Apply the shared 150ms color-transition utility to these five links and narrow `transition-property` from `all` to the properties that change.

## Mobile header: Sign Up is hidden at 390px with no replacement navigation

**Labels:** `bug`, `mobile`, `design`

### Steps to reproduce

1. Open `https://pixtaffy.com/` at 1280×800 and note the `PixTaffy`, `Log In`, and `Sign Up` header controls.
2. Resize to 390×844.
3. Inspect the visible controls and look for a replacement menu or navigation control.

### Expected

Contract gaps **G-16 / G-12** state that there is **“no governing rule — observed value recorded”** for the anonymous marketing surface and site IA. For comparison, the measured desktop header exposes `PixTaffy`, `Log In`, and `Sign Up`.

### Actual

At 390×844, the visible-control sweep has 11 entries and omits `Sign Up`, although the button remains in the DOM and `controlCount` remains 13. No hamburger or menu control was measured; `navLinks` remains the same three-anchor set as desktop. The mobile body text starts `PixTaffy\nLog In\nYOUR BRAND'S NEW CREATIVE CREW…`, while desktop includes `Sign Up` between `Log In` and the hero text.

### Screenshot

`mobile-home.png`

### Suggested fix (non-binding hunch)

Keep `Sign Up` visible in the mobile header or provide a clearly discoverable mobile navigation control that exposes the same action.
